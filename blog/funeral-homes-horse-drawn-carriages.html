How Funeral Homes and Horse-Drawn Carriages Work Together

A behind-the-scenes look at how we partner with funeral homes across Texas — and why coordinating through your funeral director makes everything easier for everyone.

Most families who are drawn to the idea of a horse-drawn funeral procession have never arranged one before. They may not know exactly how it works, how it fits into the existing service, or who they should call first. That uncertainty is completely understandable — and it is exactly why the relationship between Texas Funeral Carriage and the funeral homes we work with matters so much.

The short answer is this: the funeral director is the natural center of coordination, and when they are involved from the beginning, everything goes more smoothly — for the family, for us, and for everyone who is part of the service.

A Note to Funeral Directors

If you have never worked with a horse-drawn carriage service before, or if you have questions about how our service integrates with yours, please call us. We mean that genuinely — no question is too basic, no detail too small. We have been doing this for over a decade and we would rather spend twenty minutes on the phone walking you through the process than have any part of a family's service feel uncertain or uncoordinated.

We are happy to come to you. Whether you want to sit down with your team over breakfast or lunch, walk through how the logistics work, or simply have a conversation about the kinds of families who tend to request this service — we will show up. We will bring the food. And we will make sure you leave the meeting feeling completely confident about offering this to your families.

A Seamless Addition to Any Service

In practice, adding a horse-drawn hearse to a funeral service is simpler than most funeral directors expect. We typically meet at the cemetery entrance, where the casket is transferred from the motor hearse to our horse-drawn hearse. We then lead the procession to the gravesite at a walking pace. The motor hearse remains part of the service — our role is the final mile, the part that the family will remember most vividly.

We coordinate directly with whoever is managing the service on your end. We can communicate with your team about timing and ETAs without ever disturbing the family during the service. The goal is to be invisible in our logistics and unmistakable in our presence.

How the Coordination Works

Funeral director contacts us to book the service and confirm details. We discuss the cemetery location, route, timing, and any special requests. On the day of service, we meet at the designated point — typically the cemetery entrance. The casket transfers from the motor hearse to ours, we lead the procession to the gravesite, and we handle everything from there. The funeral director remains in charge of the overall service throughout.

A Distinctive Offering — Under Your Name

Some funeral homes choose to present our service as part of their own offerings. We are fully supportive of this arrangement. The family's experience is what matters most, and if a funeral home wants to offer a horse-drawn procession as one of their services — coordinated, presented, and delivered seamlessly — we will work quietly in the background to make that possible. We carry our own insurance, handle our own equipment, and bring our own expertise. The funeral home brings the relationship with the family and the professional context that makes everything feel cohesive.

This also makes practical business sense. A horse-drawn procession is a distinctive offering that very few funeral homes in Texas can provide. It sets you apart, it adds genuine value to families who would otherwise have no way of knowing this option exists, and it creates a memorable experience that families talk about — and recommend — for years.

We Can Also Be Your Backup

Funeral homes occasionally face busy periods — multiple services running simultaneously, a motor hearse in for maintenance, or simply an unexpectedly high volume of arrangements at once. In these situations, we can serve as a seamless alternative. Rather than renting a vehicle or turning a family away from an upgraded service, a call to us can solve the problem elegantly — and give the family an experience that exceeds what they originally expected.

We are available across Texas and travel statewide. If you are managing a service outside your usual area or coordinating with a cemetery in another city, we can discuss coverage and logistics directly.

For Families: Let Your Funeral Director Handle It

If you are a family member reading this and you want a horse-drawn procession for your loved one — the best first step is to mention it to your funeral director. They can contact us directly, work out the logistics, and integrate everything into the service plan. This takes the coordination entirely off your plate at a time when you have more than enough to manage.

A funeral director who has worked with us before will know exactly what to ask. One who hasn't can call us and we will walk them through everything. Either way, the result is the same — a seamless, dignified procession that honors your loved one without adding any stress to your day.

"The families who find the experience most meaningful are almost always the ones whose funeral director handled every detail in advance. On the day itself, they simply showed up — and were present."

We're an Open Book

We do not have a complicated process, a long list of requirements, or a steep learning curve. We have horses, we have carriages, we have ten-plus years of experience serving families across Texas, and we genuinely love what we do. If you are a funeral director who has never worked with us and you are curious — call us. If you have worked with us before and want to expand how you offer our services — call us. If you are a family with questions you are not sure who to ask — call us, or ask your funeral director to reach out on your behalf.

We are easy to work with. We show up. And we never take for granted what it means to be trusted with the final mile of someone's life.
